# FDIC branch deposits × OT: killed at burn-in (negative result)

Pre-registered one-year-ahead forecast of the spread of branch deposits inside each US state (FDIC Summary of
Deposits): for each state and year, the equal-weighted empirical law of `ln(branch deposits) − ln(national median)`
over non-main-office branches with positive deposits, 50 states + DC. Two OT methods (a pooled Wasserstein
autoregression around the yearly barycenter, and a Wasserstein regression on state deposit growth) against simple
baselines.

**Status: `KILLED_AT_BURNIN`. No out-of-sample run, no new runs.** On the 99-point grid the burn-in (1994–2003;
next-year targets 1995–2003) median next-year squared W₂ distance was 0.0078 (0.0078208250), below the median
half-sample noise floor of 0.0196 (0.0195766804); ratio 0.3995. Every one of the 9 target years was below its own
floor. K1 and K3 (shape term, 0.0036937468 vs 0.0093507007) fired; K2 did not (shape share 0.4200 > 0.15).
Planning power of the primary test was 0.0705 with T = 22 test years (2004–2025). No replacement object is proposed.
