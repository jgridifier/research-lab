Inputs and preprocessing

  • Weekly (Wednesday) 2018-05-09 to 2026-09-23, 437 weeks after filling 2 missing weeks by linear interpolation of the level: NY Fed primary-dealer UST financing fails to deliver, OFR DVP repo outstanding, OFR tri-party volume (lab copy /workspace/ms-1114/q1/weekly.csv).
  • Monthly 2013-01 to 2025-12: SEC Form PF tab 8.27 secured borrowing from prime brokers and from reverse repo; monthly means of fails, tri-party volume and DVP outstanding where available (monthly.csv). Prime broker × DVP starts 2018-06 (91 months, only 6 segments).
  • Transform: first difference of the log, standardized. Aggregate public data only; no private rows.

What we ran

  1. Home-field reproduction on SYNTHETIC data: PSI recovers a planted delay and ignores mixed independent sources (200 runs each).
  2. PSI, Holm over 7 pairs, with the usual tools (VAR Granger, cross-correlation peak) alongside.
  3. Stability: first vs second half, and rolling 104-week / 60-month windows.
  4. Size on our sampling: 1,000 matched-persistence pairs, independent and with a shared shock, for weekly fails × DVP and monthly prime broker × reverse repo.
  5. Walk-forward benchmark and a speed test. Files: runs/2026-11-24/psi/; pre-registration runs/2026-11-24-build/PREREG.md (merged before the runs).

How to read the main chart

  1. Left picture: the usual rolling correlation, one panel per pair. It says how tightly the two move, never who leads.
  2. Right picture: one bar per window. Height = PSI (positive: the first series in the panel title leads). Blue/orange = significant at the 5% t-level for that window's number of segments; grey = not.
  3. Almost all bars are grey: with 3–4 segments per window nothing short of an enormous PSI is significant. Treat a coloured bar as a prompt, not a finding.

What it means for a decision-maker

On the public funding data we have, no series reliably runs ahead of another. The two links the standard test flagged (fails ahead of tri-party volume; reverse repo ahead of prime-broker borrowing) are what that test also reports about 6–13% of the time for pairs we know are unrelated, so they are not a basis for a trading or monitoring rule. The method itself is also cautious at our short sample sizes, so this is "no evidence", not "evidence of none".

Where it breaks

  • Short samples: 6–15 segments make the jackknife conservative (size 0.6–4.1%). Rolling windows hold only 3–4 segments.
  • First vs second half disagree (prime broker × tri-party z = −3.11 then +0.35), so even a "significant" result here would not be stable.
  • The stress window 2020-02..05 cannot be isolated: the first rolling window already spans it.
  • Two-way feedback cancels the phase slope. Lead-lag is not causal direction in nonlinear systems (a 2024 comparison with conditional mutual information, abstract read).
  • Parity with mne-connectivity was missed; see the engineering finding above.
