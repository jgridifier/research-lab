2 · Math · Effort L · ★ Top pick
A share-space variance ratio that isn't identically zero

The official VR cannot be used on shares. Build a compensatory-dynamics metric in ilr or clr coordinates, plus a rank-shift null that does not wrap trending series.

Math sketch. With clr shares \(c_{it}=\log s_{it}-\tfrac1N\sum_k\log s_{kt}\), \(\sum_i c_{it}=0\) again, so use an ilr basis or a pairwise log-ratio variance ratio, \(\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Var}(\log s_i/s_j)\) against its block-shift (no wrap) null.

Test. On simulated 40-quarter panels with a market factor: size in [0.02, 0.08] and power ≥ 0.80 for a planted sticky swap; the official VR on shares stays 0.
