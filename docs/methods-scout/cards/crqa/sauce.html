A Rust lag-profile engine with the shift null built in

The categorical lag profile is \(O(n\cdot L)\), but crqa builds the full \(n\times n\) plot each call. A Rust core with a Python binding would run the 999-shift null in one pass, with crqa 2.1.0 as the oracle.

Math sketch. One-hot encode states, \(A_{ts}=\mathbf 1[a_t=s]\); then \(\mathrm{DRP}(k) = \frac{1}{n-|k|}\sum_s\sum_t A_{ts}B_{t+k,s}\), a sum of per-state cross-correlations, computable for all shifts at once by FFT.

Test. Exact parity (difference 0) with drpfromts on all 8 weekly and 12 monthly profiles, and the full weekly null in < 1 s (R today: 143 s on 8 workers).

An order-aware CRQA test with familywise lag control and persistence-matched surrogates

Full-plot RR is order-blind and DET barely moves. Build statistics that depend on alignment (\(rr_0\), the lag profile, a lead–lag contrast), with max-T control over lags and windows, and a stationary-bootstrap or AR-matched null instead of a wrap-around shift.

Math sketch. \(T = \max_{w,k} \big(\mathrm{DRP}_w(k) - \mu_{w,k}\big)/\sigma_{w,k}\) over windows \(w\) and lags \(k\), with \(\mu,\sigma\) from surrogates that match each series' persistence; reject where the observed statistic exceeds the 95th percentile of the surrogate \(T\).

Test. Size 5% ± 1.5 points on 2,000 simulated independent AR(1) pairs at our weekly persistence; power ≥ 0.8 for a planted same-week coupling of the 2021–22 size. The circular shift is the baseline to beat.

Joint "stress-state" recurrence across all four Q1 legs

Multidimensional CRQA on top-quintile stress states of fails, the spread, Form PF repo growth and CFTC TFF leveraged-fund positioning (Q1.5): do the legs co-visit stress in March 2020, March 2023 and late 2025, or come apart?

Math sketch. Joint recurrence \(J_t = \prod_{\ell=1}^4 \mathbf 1[\text{leg } \ell \text{ in top quintile at } t]\); episode statistic \(\sum_{t\in E} J_t\) vs a block-shift null per leg.

Test. A pre-registered episode list, with joint recurrence in each episode against a block-shift null at p < 0.05. An honest miss counts.
