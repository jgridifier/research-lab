Q1.1 · Lead / lag

Fails and the spread: same week, no leader. Form PF reverse-repo growth leads DVP volume growth by about 4 months (reference month).

Q1.2 · Co-movement in stress

The fails–spread coupling was absent in the late-2025 fails spike, while fails and DVP growth were co-recurrent then: a disconnect in one leg, not the other.

Q1.3 · Does the relationship change?

Yes: on 2019 to mid-2025, peak through mid-2022, off after July 2025. CCM (11-15) later gave the link a direction (fails → spread).
