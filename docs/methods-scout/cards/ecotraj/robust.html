Not graded Home-field grading started with the 2026-11-16 edition. Sourced facts:

  • Paper: De Cáceres et al. (2019), Ecological Monographs, the ETA framework (lengths, angles, directionality, segment and trajectory distances).
  • Package: ecotraj on CRAN (1.2.2 used as the oracle), R only before ecotraj-py.
  • Our replication: ecotraj-py reproduces the scout's numbers exactly (A dir 0.577301, E4 dir 0.431413, A residual-increment dir 0.595092; sign-flip p exact on R's draws) and 1,260/1,280 randomized checks.
  • Known limits: no built-in significance test for directionality in ecotraj; the sign-flip null and the increment split are lab extensions, checked by simulation, not taken from the paper.
