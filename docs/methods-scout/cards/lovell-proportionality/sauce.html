proprlite.py (NumPy): clr, VLR, φ, ρ, vectorised pairwise ρ, level- and increment-partial ρ, the exact cyclic-shift p-value and BH.

Partial proportionality given the market, with a persistence-aware null

Remove the market from each part's clr increments (not levels), then compute ρ on the residual increments, with an exact relative cyclic-shift null.

Math sketch. \(e = (I - Z(Z^\top Z)^{-1}Z^\top)\,\Delta u\) with \(Z = [1, \Delta X]\); \(\rho_{\text{partial}} = 2\operatorname{Cov}(e^u,e^v)/(\operatorname{Var}e^u+\operatorname{Var}e^v)\).

Test (set in advance) and result. Size 2–8% at T = 40 and 62 when the pair shares only the market; power ≥ 80% at T = 62 on a planted swap. Increment partial: 6.5% / 6.2%, power 99.0% (pass). Level partial (11-12 prototype): 12.7% / 13.3% (fails size). Raw ρ: 100% (fails). With a noisy market proxy all three fail.
