Inputs and preprocessing

  • Lab copies of public files (built 11-14/11-17): NY Fed financing fails (weekly, Wednesday), OFR DVP overnight and tri-party overnight rates and DVP outstanding (daily → weekly), CFTC TFF leveraged-fund net short in UST 2Y + 5Y + 10Y futures (to 2025-12-30; later weeks missing in the lab copy).
  • Window 2018-05-09..2026-09-23, 436 weeks; single-week gaps interpolated.
  • Storm rule fixed before any loop was computed (see The math): 17 qualifying peaks → 13 storms after the limb and merge rules. The 2019 storm is a merge of overlapping peaks and runs from 2019-01-30 to 2019-10-16.
  • Daily leg: OFR final-vintage daily series 2018-05-08..2026-06-30 (1,947 days).

What we ran

  1. Home field: Lloyd et al. Fig. 1 (ratio index 0.66, 4, 0.33, −0.66; new index 0.5 at both levels with ratio 1.25 and 5) reproduced exactly; sine loops signed correctly.
  2. Per storm: HI at 5% discharge steps and FI; mean over storms vs 4,999 circular shifts; Holm over 3 series.
  3. Leave-one-storm-out; post-hoc quarter-end masking; daily repo-only storms; power simulation.
  4. Standard tools on the same weeks: lagged correlations (levels and changes), ARDL by BIC both ways, SARIMAX(1,0,1) with lagged spread; walk-forward 1-week-ahead from 2022-07-06 with and without the pre-registered rising-limb term.
How to read the main chart (the loop grid)
  1. Each panel is one fails storm. Horizontal axis: fails, rescaled to 0–1 within the storm. Vertical: the DVP − tri-party spread, rescaled the same way.
  2. Orange points are weeks while fails rise, teal while they fall; arrows show time order.
  3. If the teal path sits above the orange one, the spread was higher after the fails peak than before: an anticlockwise loop, negative HI (red titles: HI ≤ −0.2).
  4. The pattern to see: many loops close with a late jump in the spread on the falling side; several of those jumps are in late December or around quarter-ends.
What it means for a decision-maker

When Treasury settlement fails spike, the gap between the two main repo rates tends to widen after the fails peak rather than before, so in these episodes the repo-rate signal does not give early warning of fails. But most of that pattern comes from fails episodes happening to end near quarter- and year-ends, when repo rates jump anyway. Funding volumes and hedge-fund futures positions show no consistent before/after pattern. Public aggregates; descriptive; not a trading signal.

Where it breaks (seen in our runs)

  • Calendar confound. Quarter-end weeks carry most of the loop (−0.17 → −0.08 when masked).
  • Few storms. 13 weekly episodes; one long merged 2019 storm with many wiggles.
  • Storm rule matters. Hydrology papers flag event delineation as a main source of disagreement; we fixed ours in advance but did not test alternatives.
  • Daily trigger had to change. The pre-registered 1.5× ratio rule is undefined for a spread that can be zero or negative; we used excess over the trailing median (labelled).
  • Positioning gap. TFF in the lab copy stops at 2025-12-30, so the last two storms have no positioning loop.
