Q1.1 · Lead/lag

Inside fails storms the repo spread moves after fails (anticlockwise; mean index −0.17, p = 0.042, Holm 0.126), which a full-sample ARDL or cross-correlation cannot express. DVP volume and leveraged-fund positioning show no before/after pattern. Form PF is quarterly or monthly and has too few points per storm for this method.

Q1.2 · Co-movement vs coincidence in stress

Most of the spread's late jump is calendar: with quarter-end weeks masked, −0.08 (p = 0.35). The March 2020 storm is almost loop-free (+0.01); the late-2024 and year-end storms carry the anticlockwise shape. The calendar-locked null (top pick) settles it.

Q1.5 · Positioning vs funding

No loop between leveraged-fund net short and fails across 11 storms (+0.03, p = 0.75). The New-use avenue looks at the ECA unwind weeks instead.
