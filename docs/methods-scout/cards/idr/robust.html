Not graded Home-field grading started with the 2026-11-16 edition. What the paper and the 11-14 page record:

  • Theory: Henzi, Ziegel & Gneiting, JRSS-B 83(5):963–993 (2021). The abstract states that IDR learns conditional distributions that are calibrated and simultaneously optimal relative to comprehensive classes of loss functions, subject to isotonicity; isotonic quantile and binary regression are special cases. §2.4 gives consistency.
  • Recommended use in its home field: the authors recommend IDR as a generic benchmark for probabilistic forecasts because it needs no tuning, and report it competitive with state-of-the-art postprocessing on precipitation forecasts (§5).
  • Follow-ons on the 11-14 watchlist: EasyUQ (SIAM Review 2024), distributional single-index models (JASA 2023), survival IDR (arXiv 2026).
  • Implementation: CRAN isodistrreg 0.6.0 (2026-08-21) with a Rust core built by Cargo.
