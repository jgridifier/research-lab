Inputs and preprocessing

  • CFTC Traders in Financial Futures (leveraged-fund net short in 2y, 5y, 10y, an unweighted sum, not DV01-weighted), NY Fed financing fails, OFR DVP and tri-party data.
  • Weekly differences (log differences for volumes and fails). Two holiday weeks without a fails release interpolated. The lab's TFF copy stops at 2025-12-30, so the panel ends there.
  • Embedding \(E\) per series by simplex skill (first half fit, second half test); 4-week exclusion radius.

What we ran

  1. Own-dynamics checks: simplex and S-map skill for each series (is it predictable, is it nonlinear?).
  2. Convergence curves for 10 directions (100 random libraries per point, 10 points).
  3. 200 driver-shift surrogates per direction at \(L = 350\); Holm across directions.
  4. Lag scan −4…+4 weeks; linear Granger as the plain baseline; the same analysis on each half.
  5. Full run: 148 s single-threaded.
How to read the main chart (convergence curves)
  1. x = library size \(L\); y = cross-map skill \(\rho\). Each panel is one direction (driver → responder).
  2. A link needs two things: the curve rises as \(L\) grows, and it ends above the grey band (below the null's 95th percentile).
  3. Fails → spread and positioning → spread rise (+0.24 and +0.19 from \(L\) = 20 to 350) and clear the band.
  4. Flat curves inside the band (positioning ↔ fails) mean no detectable coupling.
What it means for a decision-maker

The DVP–tri-party rate spread behaves like the "listener": its week-to-week moves carry traces of settlement fails and of leveraged-fund positioning, more than the reverse. Fails and positioning are not linked to each other, even though a standard linear test says they are; that test is probably picking up shared calendar effects. These links are real but modest and sit right at the edge of significance. Descriptive only.

Where it breaks (seen in our runs)

  • Noise: CCM assumes near-deterministic dynamics; ours are noisy weekly differences with \(\rho\) of 0.2–0.3.
  • p-value floor: 200 surrogates cap p at 0.005; after Holm the two links sit exactly at 0.05.
  • Unpredictable driver: positioning changes are nearly unpredictable from their own past (\(\rho\approx0.07\)), so they can only be tested as a driver.
  • Regime change: links into DVP volume exist only after mid-2022, so full-sample numbers mix two regimes.
  • Circular shift wraps the end of each series onto its start.

What we'd do next

Top pick (Engineering): reuse the responder's neighbour sets across surrogates, so 10,000 surrogates cost little and the p-value floor drops. Then rolling CCM on the late-2025 switch-off seen by CRQA.
