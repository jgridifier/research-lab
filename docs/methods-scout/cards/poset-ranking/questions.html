Q3.1 · Cross-section

Yes, with a weight-free answer: where one firm is worse on drawdown, pre-tax income and loan losses together, it paid out less in 72% of 2,203 pairs (p = 0.0002; next-year payouts 66%). This adds to IDR (11-14) and PIM (11-16), which used one indicator or a fitted model at a time.

Q3.2 · Buffer versus path

Path. In 2021–2025 pairs where the stress path and the SCB disagree (175, mostly both at the 2.5% floor), payouts follow the path 79% of the time (p = 0.001); SCB order alone gets 57%.

Q3.3 · Year-to-year movement

The stress ordering is stable (74% of dominance relations persist, 2% reverse); the payout ranking is less so after 2020 (Kendall τ 0.40–0.69 vs 0.53–0.84 before), consistent with the 11-17 STATIS era split.

New for Q3 · which indicator

In the 55% of pairs that are trade-offs, payouts follow pre-tax income (0.64) and run against the loan-loss rate (0.41). A composite averages these to 0.55. Q3.4 (Adverse vs severely adverse gap) and Q3.5 (dividends vs buybacks) are not answered here; Q3.5 is the New-use avenue.
