Two borrowed tools, each with a full research card. ● Q1: 26% of NY Fed financing-fails spike weeks come one week after a big leveraged-fund unwind in Treasury futures, vs about 10% by chance (circular-shift p = 0.008). Big builds: 10%, p = 0.54. A plain cross-correlation shows only r = −0.16. ● Q3: the joint stress-test–payout correlation pattern splits into 2013–19 and 2020–25 eras (exact permutation p = 0.023), best cut at 2021 (exploratory). No single correlation change survives familywise control.

The short version

  • Event coincidence analysis (ECA) · climate-impact science · Q1 · recommend · home field: promising but thin. Same/previous-week unwind → fails link survives Holm over 48 pre-registered tests (38% vs 18%, Holm p = 0.0096) and passes all five stability checks. Monthly Form PF borrowing spikes show nothing (honest miss). The Poisson test over-rejects on our clustered series (9.2% at nominal 5%). Top pick: conditional ECA (New use, effort S).
  • STATIS multi-table analysis · sensory science / ecology · Q3 · interesting · home field: well established. Era split p = 0.023 (modified RV 0.016), moderate under leave-one-year-out (9 of 13 below 0.05). The pre-registered "which year departs" bootstrap failed because RV is biased in small samples. Top pick: reliability-corrected RV (Math, effort M).
