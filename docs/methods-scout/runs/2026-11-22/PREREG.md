## Method 1 — Reverberation-mapping lag estimation: ICCF with random-subset-selection (RSS) centroid distributions, plus ZDCF (astrostatistics), Q1
Home field: AGN reverberation mapping (Gaskell & Peterson 1987 ICCF; Peterson et al. 1998 PASP 110:660 FR/RSS; Alexander 1997 ZDCF). Implementations: PyCCF V2.2 (Sun, Grier & Peterson; ASCL 1805.032; Python 2 code, minimally ported to Python 3 here as the oracle), pyZDCF 1.0.3 (Jankov; port of Alexander's Fortran), plus an independent vectorized NumPy ICCF.
Home-field reproduction:
- R1: PyCCF sample light curves (sample_lc1/2.dat): our Python-3 port of the original and our NumPy ICCF reproduce the shipped sample_ccf_yap.dat (101 lags, printed to 5 decimals) to <= 1e-5; NumPy vs original port parity <= 1e-12 on the sample and on 50 random uneven pairs; CCCD median within 2 days of the shipped sample_centtab_yap.dat median (500 draws, different RNG).
- R2: Yu et al. (2020) direction: on DRW (OU) simulations with a planted lag and the sample's cadence, the FR+RSS CCCD overestimates the true lag scatter (eta = sigma_est/sigma_obs > 1). 200 sims.
Data (public aggregates, lab copies on the box): SEC Form PF tab 8.27 monthly borrowing (prime broker, reverse repo; 2013-01..2025-12; /workspace/ms-1114/q1/monthly.csv); NY Fed primary-dealer UST (ex-TIPS) financing fails to deliver PDFTD-USTET weekly 2013-04-03..2025-12-31 (/workspace/research/drive_inv/data/fails_weekly.csv); OFR tri-party transaction volume REPO-TRI_TV_TOT (daily from 2014-08-22 -> weekly Wednesday mean), DVP outstanding and DVP - tri-party overnight spread (weekly 2018-05..; /workspace/ms-1114/q1/weekly.csv). Times in days; ICCF lag grid -365..+365 d, step 7 d; positive lag = market series lags Form PF.
Transform (primary): log level (spread in bp level), linearly detrended over each pair's overlap. Sensitivity: 12-month / 52-week log change (spread: 52-week change). No measurement errors exist for these series, so FR is off and RSS only is used (said plainly; mcmode=RSS).
Pairs (Holm over 5): P1 PF prime-broker (PB) x fails; P2 PB x tri-party volume; P3 PB x DVP outstanding; P4 PB x DVP - tri spread; P5 PF reverse repo x DVP outstanding (prior lab claim, CRQA 11-14: PF reverse repo leads DVP by about 4 months).
- C1 (Q1.1/Q1.2 lead-lag): ICCF r(tau), statistic r* = max |r| over the window (sign kept); centroid lag tau_cent (r > 0.8 r_peak, as PyCCF) on the sign-adjusted pair; RSS CCCD 2,000 draws -> median and 68%/95% intervals. Nulls (matched persistence): (a) independent OU/AR(1) fitted to each detrended series at its native cadence, (b) amplitude-adjusted Fourier phase-randomized surrogates of each series; 1,000 each; p = share of null r* >= observed |r*|. Finding = Holm p < 0.05 under BOTH nulls AND 68% CCCD interval excludes 0. Standard tools: monthly-averaged CCF of the same detrended series with Bartlett +-1.96/sqrt(n) bands (lag of max |r|; "significant" if outside band), and monthly Granger (VAR, BIC lag <= 6) on log differences.
- C2 (Q1.3 stability): P1, P2 split 2013/14-2019 vs 2020-2025; P3, P4, P5 split 2018-05..2021-12 vs 2022-01..2025-12. Report r*, tau_cent, CCCD 68% per half. Sensitivity transform (12-month change) for all pairs.
- C3 (size and calibration on our sampling): 1,000 independent OU pairs matched to P1 (and P3) persistence and sampling. Rejection rate of (i) Bartlett bands, any of 25 monthly lags (-12..12); (ii) Bartlett on monthly log differences; (iii) home-field detection rule (r_peak > 0.5 and 95% CCCD excludes 0); (iv) our OU null (should be ~5%) and phase null. Pass for the method: (iv) within [0.03, 0.07]. Plus planted lag (y2 = y1 shifted +120 d + OU noise, SNR 1, our cadence): bias of tau_cent and eta = sigma_CCCD / sigma_obs (200 sims).
- C4 (ZDCF): pyZDCF on P1 and P5 at weekly/monthly sampling (times in integer days); peak lag vs ICCF tau_cent. Descriptive.
- B (benchmark, low priority, walk-forward): target monthly change in log PF PB borrowing, one month ahead, test 2019-01..2025-12, expanding window: OLS on lagged market regressor, ARDL (own lags, BIC <= 3), ARDL + market regressor at lag k (k = round(tau_cent / 30.4) from ICCF on the training window only, refit each January, k >= 1 enforced), SARIMAX(1,0,1), SARIMAX(1,0,1)+X at the same k, zero change. Regressor = P1 fails (monthly mean log change). Promising: ICCF-lag ARDL RMSE >= 2% below own-lag ARDL with DM p < 0.10. Descriptive study; this is not the Y-9C forecast study.
- E (engineering): NumPy ICCF + RSS vs original PyCCF loop (py3 port), 2,000 draws on P1: parity of the CCF and wall time.
Visual: ICCF curve with CCCD histogram and null band (method) vs monthly CCF bars with Bartlett bands (standard).

## Method 2 — L-moment regional frequency analysis (Hosking & Wallis; discordancy D_i, heterogeneity H, Z-DIST, index-flood quantiles) (hydrology), Q2 (SYNTHETIC mock ZBB only)
Home field: hydrology / flood and rainfall frequency (Hosking & Wallis 1993 WRR; 1997/2005 book). Implementation: R lmomRFA 3.8 (Fortran core) + lmom 3.3; independent NumPy port of sample L-moments, D_i and V.
Home-field reproduction: R1: lmomRFA regtst on Cascades vs CASCADES.OUT (Hosking LMOMENTS Fortran, StatLib): D_i and weighted means exact to printed precision; kappa parameters to printed precision; H1 = 0.62, H2 = -1.49, H3 = -2.37 within +-0.25 (500 sims, different RNG); Z values within +-0.3; GNO parameters exact to printed precision. R2: NumPy port parity with lmomRFA (L-moment ratios, D_i, V) to 1e-10 on Cascades-like and on our data.
Data: SYNTHETIC Corning_mock (FY2020-25) and MorganStanley_mock (FY2021-25) from /workspace/q2-mock-zbb/output. "Site" = program; record = yearly budget-miss ratio x = actual_usd / budget_usd (positive, about 1). Keep programs with >= 4 years. Raking multiplies actual and budget by the same factor, so x is raking-invariant. Answer key for scoring only: the generator's per-program noise sigma (re-derived by re-running the generator's deterministic draws to the history step; validated by exact match of log x to history.csv), ground_truth mismatch list. True quantile: log x ~ N(0.02 - 0.255 sigma^2, 1.2207 sigma) (from the generator's formulas).
Regions: (A) stated function; (B) stated discretionary x size band (mean actual < 3m, 3-8m, >= 8m; HW-style "site characteristics", observable); (C) text clusters (k-means k=30 on TF-IDF of descriptions with numeric/history sentences scrubbed as on 11-19); (D) random regions of matched sizes (null); (O) oracle: true sigma class (answer key, reference only). Regions with < 5 sites merged into the nearest by size.
- Q1 (Q2.3 real vs noise, homogeneity): H1 per region for A-D and O (500 sims). Expect O: H1 mostly < 1 (calibration on 4-6-year records); D (random) mixes sigma classes. Standard tool: Brown-Forsythe (Levene, median) test of equal variance of log x across programs within region. Report share of regions "heterogeneous" (H1 >= 2; Levene p < 0.05) and a size/power check: 200 simulated homogeneous / two-class regions with our record lengths and N. Pass: H1 power >= Levene power + 0.05 at matched size, or H1 size closer to nominal.
- Q2 (Q2.1 borrowing strength): per-program 90th-percentile budget-miss ratio q90 and sigma. Estimators: (i) at-site empirical (type 7), (ii) at-site lognormal (mean + 1.2816 sd of log x), (iii) RFA index-flood (regions B; regional GNO growth curve x at-site mean; distribution choice by Z-DIST reported), (iv) RFA on regions A, C, (v) standard pooling: category-pooled lognormal SD by stated function, (vi) James-Stein-style shrinkage of log sd toward the region-B mean (normal-theory EB). Score: RMSE of log q90 vs truth, both mocks. Pass: (iii) >= 20% below (ii) and <= (v).
- Q3 (Q2.2 text vs numbers): D_i within region B; AUC of D_i (and of at-site L-CV z within region) vs planted stated_mandatory_actually_discretionary (true sigma >= 0.16 vs stated mandatory peers 0.065/0.085), among stated-mandatory programs. Comparators: within-region z of at-site sd of log x; per-site chi-square variance ratio vs region pooled variance. Pass: D_i AUC >= best comparator + 0.03. Also flags at D_i >= 3 and their precision.
- B (benchmark, walk-forward, time-indexed): bound for the last year's x (FY2025) from earlier years only: 90% upper bound; methods (ii) at-site lognormal, (iii) RFA index-flood (B), (v) category-pooled, OLS (pooled log x on stated features + residual quantile by region B), pooled ARDL(1) on log x by region B (residual 90% quantile), per-program SARIMAX(1,0,0) (statsmodels; where it fails, fall back to the at-site lognormal and say how often). Score: coverage (target 0.90) and pinball loss at 0.9. Promising: RFA pinball <= 0.95 x at-site lognormal with coverage in [0.86, 0.94].
Visual: L-moment ratio diagram (L-skew vs L-kurt, theoretical curves) and L-CV vs record length funnel coloured by discordancy (method) vs the standard grouped boxplot of budget miss by function.
