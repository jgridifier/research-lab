## Method 1 — Liang–Kleeman information flow (LKIF), Q1
Home field: atmosphere/ocean dynamics and climate science (Liang 2014 PRE 90:052150; normalized Liang 2015 PRE 92:022126; multivariate Liang 2021 Entropy 23:679). Implementation: PyPI LK_Info_flow 3.1.2.1 (Rong & Liang), plus an independent NumPy port of eq. (4)/(14) for parity.
Home-field reproduction: (R1) Liang 2021 Sec. 5.1 VAR network (A, alpha as printed; b_ii = 1, 10,000 steps): recover exactly the 7 designed edges (3->1, 1->2, 6->2, 2->3, 5->4, 4->5, 6->5) at 90% and the printed normalized flows |tau6->2| = 13.2%, |tau6->5| = 12.5%, tau4->5 = 2.4%, tau5->4 = 8.8% within +-2 pp (our random draw differs from theirs); (R2) same with b_ii = 100 and with n = 500; (R3) independent port parity with the package to 1e-10.
Derivation check (pre-stated): in the linear estimator the package's asymptotic z for T_{j->i} equals the VAR(1) OLS t-statistic of a_ij (the |C_ij/C_ii| factor cancels). Verify numerically on our data.
Data (public aggregates, lab copies): weekly (Wednesday) NY Fed primary-dealer financing fails (UST), OFR DVP and tri-party volumes and the DVP - tri-party overnight spread, CFTC TFF leveraged-fund net short UST 2Y+5Y+10Y futures (ends 2025-12-30), FRED VIXCLS (Wednesday close) as a common-driver control. Series: dl_fails, d_spread, dl_dvp, dl_tri, d_lev, d_vix, standardized; 2018-05-16..2025-12-31. Monthly: SEC Form PF tab 8.27 (prime broker, reverse repo, total borrowing; log change), monthly mean fails, spread, DVP; 2018-06..2025-12.
- L1 (Q1.1/Q1.5 causal graph): 6-variable LKIF, lag 1. Edge p-values from (i) the package asymptotic test, (ii) a circular-shift surrogate of the source series (4,999 shifts; statistic |T|), Holm over 30 directed edges. Standard tools: VAR(1) Wald (conditional Granger) for each edge, VAR(BIC) Granger, pairwise Granger (lag 1). Prior hypotheses from this lab's runs: H1 d_lev -> dl_fails (ECA 11-17), H2 dl_fails -> d_spread (CCM 11-15). Report for each significant edge the normalized flow tau and its sign (positive = raises the target's uncertainty, negative = damps it), which Granger does not give. Finding = surrogate Holm p < 0.05.
- L2 (Q1.3 stability): split at 2022-03-02 (first hike); rolling 104-week windows, step 4; edges kept in both halves (surrogate p < 0.05 each). Late 2025: 5-variable system without positioning through 2026-09-23 to see whether fails->spread flow switches off after mid-2025 (CRQA 11-14 saw the coupling stop).
- L3 (size on our data, matched persistence): simulate from the fitted VAR(1) with the tested edge set to zero, residual rows resampled by stationary block bootstrap (mean block 8), n as observed, 1,000 sims for H1 and H2 edges: rejection rate at 5% for asymptotic LKIF, surrogate LKIF (199 shifts) and Granger F. Pass: size in [0.03, 0.07].
- L4 (Q1.1 monthly Form PF): 6-variable LKIF on monthly series, lag 1 (sensitivity: AIC lag); surrogate null (4,999), Holm over 30. Compare with Granger and with the 11-14 CRQA "Form PF reverse repo leads DVP by ~4 months".
- B (benchmark, low priority, walk-forward): 1-week-ahead dl_fails from 2022-07-06 on, expanding window: ARDL(own 2 lags), ARDL + LKIF-selected parents (selected on the training window only), ARDL + Granger-selected parents, SARIMAX(1,0,1), random walk on log fails. Promising: LKIF parents >= 1% lower RMSE than own-lag ARDL with DM p < 0.10.
- Timing: LKIF vs statsmodels VAR Granger loop for d = 6, 30, 100 simulated series (n = 400 and 3,000).

## Method 2 — Necessary Condition Analysis (NCA; ceiling envelopment), Q3
Home field: management/organization science (Dul 2016 ORM; permutation test Dul, van der Laan & Kuik 2020 ORM), with roots in ecological limiting-factor / boundary-line analysis. R package NCA (CRAN). Contested: Sorjonen & Melin argue ceiling zones arise from plain correlation ("range of spuriousness"); Dul et al. reply. Implementation: NCA package + independent NumPy CE-FDH/CR-FDH port.
Home-field reproduction: (R1) NCA package nca.example (Individualism, Risk taking -> Innovation performance): CE-FDH and CR-FDH effect sizes and p values vs the values printed in the package documentation/vignette if available (else say not checked); (R2) Python port parity with the package d to 1e-8 on nca.example and on our data.
Data (public, lab's verified panel runs/2026-11-17/statis/q3_pim_panel.csv): 13 severely adverse exercises 2013–2025, 360 firm-years with payouts. Conditions oriented so higher = better: X1 = - CET1 drawdown (pp), X2 = SA pre-tax net income (% assets), X3 = - SA loan-loss rate (%), X4 = SA minimum CET1 ratio (%), X5 = - SCB (2021–2025). Outcome Y = payout/RWA (%) same calendar year; sensitivity next-year payout, dividends/RWA, net buybacks/RWA.
- N1 (Q3.1 ceilings): CE-FDH and CR-FDH effect size d per condition (pooled) with (a) NCA approximate permutation p (10,000), (b) within-year permutation p (4,999), (c) Sorjonen–Melin range of spuriousness: Gaussian copula with the observed marginals and observed Spearman rho (1,999 sims), observed d vs that distribution. Finding = d >= 0.10, p < 0.05 under (b) AND observed d above the 95th percentile of (c). Standard tools: OLS slope and R^2; quantile regression at tau = 0.90 and 0.95. Bottleneck table (condition level required for payout at 25/50/75/90% of the observed range).
- N2 (Q3.2 limiting factor): multi-condition CE-FDH ceilings; for each firm-year the binding (lowest) ceiling = limiting factor (Liebig); shares by factor; in 2021–2025 add -SCB and report its d and how often it binds vs the path conditions.
- N3 (Q3.3 stability): d per era (2013–2019 vs 2020–2025) with within-era permutation; leave-one-year-out d; bottleneck at median payout by era.
- N4 (benchmark, walk-forward upper envelope; time-indexed): test years 2015–2025, envelope fitted on earlier years only, on the best N1 condition: out-of-sample share of firm-years above (i) CE-FDH ceiling, (ii) QR tau = 0.95 line, (iii) OLS + 1.645 s, (iv) pooled ARDL(1,0) 95% upper bound (payout_t ~ payout_{t-1} + stress), (v) per-firm SARIMAX(1,0,0)+exog 95% upper bound (>= 8 prior years, else ARDL). Also mean headroom (bound - actual). Promising (pre-stated): ceiling violation rate <= 10% with mean headroom no larger than the QR line's. Descriptive question; envelopes are a benchmark, and FR Y-9C stays descriptive (no Y-9C trading forecast).
Visual: scatter with CE-FDH step ceiling and shaded empty zone vs the OLS fitted line on the same scatter; limiting-factor stacked bars by year.
