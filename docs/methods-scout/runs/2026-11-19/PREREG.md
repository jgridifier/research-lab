## Method 1 — Partial-order ranking with exact rank probabilities (netrankr; Hasse diagram technique), Q3
Home field: network science (Schoch 2018, Social Networks 54:50–60, centrality without indices) built on the environmental-chemistry Hasse diagram technique (Brüggemann et al.) and exact rank probabilities from the lattice of ideals (De Loof, De Meyer & De Baets 2006, Fundamenta Informaticae 71:309–321).
Home-field reproduction: (R1) netrankr documented 5-element example P (exact_rank_prob) and the partial_centrality vignette rank intervals on the dbces11 graph (node G [2,11], node K [3,11]); (R2) independent brute-force enumeration of all linear extensions in Python on the 5-element example and on random posets (n ≤ 9) -> parity of rank probabilities and expected ranks to 1e-12.
Data (public, the lab's verified copies; runs/2026-11-17/statis/q3_pim_panel.csv): 13 severely adverse exercises 2013–2025, 360 firm-years with payouts. Stress orientation (higher = more stressed): CET1 drawdown (pp), minus SA pre-tax net income (% assets), SA loan-loss rate (%). A dominates B in year y iff A >= B on all three and > on at least one. Payout = Y-9C payout/RWA (%), same calendar year (and next year as sensitivity).
- A1 (Q3.1 dominance test): over all within-year strict dominance pairs, concordance C = share where the more-stressed firm pays out less (ties in payout count 1/2). Null: permute payouts within year (poset fixed), 4,999 perms; one-sided (C > null). Same for next-year payout. Standard tool: Spearman of payout with (a) equal-weight z composite, (b) PC1, (c) pooled OLS fitted composite; and Kendall concordance over ALL pairs ordered by each composite. Report incomparable share per year. Finding = p < 0.05.
- A2 (rank ambiguity): exact rank probabilities per year (netrankr exact_rank_prob; MCMC fallback if infeasible, said so). Report mean rank-interval width / n and mean rank SD; rank of each composite inside the intervals (all positive-weight composites are linear extensions by construction). Walk-forward (year y+1 cross-section from fits on years <= y, 2015..2025): Spearman(predicted, realized payout/RWA) for (i) poset expected rank (no fitted weights), (ii) pooled OLS on the 3 stress variables, (iii) pooled ARDL(1,0): payout_t ~ payout_{t-1} + stress_t, (iv) SARIMAX(1,0,0) with exog = 3 stress variables per firm with >= 8 prior years, else firm's last payout. Promising (pre-stated): (i) within 0.05 mean Spearman of (ii) with zero fitted weights. Descriptive question; forecasts are only a benchmark.
- A3 (Q3.2 buffer vs path, 2021–2025): pairs comparable on the path poset where the SCB order disagrees (path-more-stressed firm has SCB <= the other's, including both at the 2.5% floor). In those pairs, which order does payout follow? Statistic: path concordance among disagreement pairs; null within-year permutation (4,999). Also SCB-only concordance among its strict pairs vs path concordance among its pairs.
- A4 (Q3.3 year to year): for firm pairs present in consecutive test years, share of dominance relations that persist / flip / become incomparable; vs Kendall tau of the equal-weight composite between the same years. Descriptive; plus relative-rank-probability matrix correlation between consecutive years.
Visual: Hasse diagram of one test year (2024, 31 firms) coloured by payout/RWA vs the standard composite bar ranking; rank-probability heat map.

## Method 2 — Phylogenetic signal and LIPA on the program hierarchy (phylosignal; Keck et al. 2016), Q2 (SYNTHETIC mock ZBB only)
Home field: phylogenetic comparative methods / community ecology (Keck, Rimet, Bouchez & Franc 2016, Ecology and Evolution 6:2774–2780; Blomberg K 2003; Pagel lambda 1999; Abouheif Cmean; benchmark Münkemüller et al. 2012 MEE).
Home-field reproduction: (R1) Brownian motion on a random 100-tip tree: mean K ~ 1 and lambda ~ 1 (Blomberg et al. 2003 expectation); white noise (star): K ~ 0, lambda ~ 0; 200 sims. (R2) phylosignal Basics vignette numbers (carnivora example) if printed values are available; otherwise say not reproduced.
Data: SYNTHETIC Corning_mock (778 balanced programs FY2020–25) and MorganStanley_mock (1,404 FY2021–25) from /workspace/q2-mock-zbb/output. Trait = per-program OLS slope of log actual_usd on year. "Label tree" = taxonomy firm > segment > subsegment > function > stated_intent > program (unit branch lengths, made ultrametric). "Text tree" = average-linkage hierarchical clustering of TF-IDF (1–2 grams) of descriptions with every sentence that mentions dollars, actuals, budget miss, years or digits REMOVED (the descriptions leak numeric history). Answer key (ground_truth.json) used only for scoring.
- P1 (Q2.4/Q2.3 global signal): K, lambda, Cmean, Moran's I (999 perms) of slope on label tree and text tree; standard tool = ANOVA R^2 of slope on function (and on segment x function x stated_intent). Also on noise-free true slopes. Descriptive: which tree carries more signal; whether noise hides it.
- P2 (Q2.2 text vs numbers): LIPA (local Moran's I, patristic proximity, 999 perms) per program on the label tree. Score: AUC of -LIPA (and of signed rule: low growth with high-growth relatives) vs planted stated_growth_actually_declining and stated_run_actually_transform; comparators: within-function peer z of slope, raw OLS slope. Pass: LIPA-based AUC >= best comparator + 0.03 on at least one mock for a growth-type mismatch.
- P3 (Q2.2 category drift): LIPA of early-window slope (first 3 years) vs late-window slope (last 3 years); drift score = LIPA_early - LIPA_late (relatives-agreement lost). Score AUC vs planted category_drift (all fields; intent separately). Comparator: |change in peer z|. Pass: AUC >= 0.60 and >= comparator + 0.03.
- P4 (benchmark, walk-forward one step): predict FY2025 log growth from data to FY2024: per-program OLS trend, pooled ARDL(1) on growth with no FE, SARIMAX(1,0,0) per program (statsmodels), and phylogenetic neighbour mean (Abouheif-proximity weighted mean of relatives' trailing growth) and their 50/50 blend with OLS. RMSE vs true FY2025 actual growth. Promising: phylo neighbour or blend RMSE >= 5% below OLS trend.
Visual: circular/fan tree with slope bars coloured by LIPA significance vs the standard grouped boxplot by function.
