One borrowed tool with a full research card. From computational neuroscience: partial information decomposition, the way neuroscientists ask whether two brain areas carry the same information about a stimulus, different information, or information that only shows up when both are read together. Here the two "brain areas" are the stress capital buffer and the stress-test drawdown, and the "stimulus" is what each bank paid out.

● Q3.2: within a test year the SCB carries 0.023 bits about payout ranks, all shared with the drawdown. The pair adds 0.097 bits of synergy, and that synergy is the SCB's dividend add-on (SCB − drawdown). OLS reads the same fact as "a higher SCB, higher payouts" (t = +5.07). ● Miss: the synergy is significant only under MMI, the measure with the most synergy; under Idep and at the measure-free lower bound its CI reaches 0 (Idep power 32% at n = 132).

The short version

  • Partial information decomposition, Gaussian-copula form (MMI and Idep) · computational neuroscience · Q3 · interesting · home field: promising but contested. Williams–Beer AND gate, Barrett's closed form and Kay & Ince's Example 4 all reproduced, but redundancy measures disagree. On 2021–2025 stress tests it explains the SCB's sign flip in OLS as synergy and traces it to the dividend add-on. After 2020, drawdown and pre-tax income say more of the same about payouts (shared 0.036 → 0.128 bits, p = 0.033). Adverse vs severely adverse adds nothing robust.
