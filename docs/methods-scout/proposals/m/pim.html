Finding 1

Stress-test pre-tax income orders payouts; drawdown adds nothing beyond it.

A bank one IQR better on severely adverse pre-tax income has the higher payout 68% of the time (Holm p = 0.02).

Finding 2

It holds where the usual regression is fragile.

Trimming the 8 most extreme payout ratios cuts the regression slope from −0.070 to −0.040. The PIM effect moves from 0.4675 to 0.4697.

Bars computed from the page's numbers: 0.040/0.070 and (0.5−0.4697)/(0.5−0.4675).

Finding 3

Before 2020 stress showed up in dividends; after, maybe buybacks.

Pre-2020 dividends: 36% per IQR (Holm p = 0.02); buybacks 49%. Post-2020 buybacks 44% (p = 0.034), which does not survive Holm.

  1. Take two banks in the same test year. Ask: how likely is bank A's payout above bank B's, given their stress numbers?
  2. That probability is the effect. 50% means the stress metric does not order payouts.
  3. Pairs are only formed within a year, so changes in the scenario and in payout norms cancel out.
  4. It reads ranks, so a near-zero income cannot dominate; it is a regression, so it gives partial effects.
  5. Fast: 49,500 pairs fit in 0.05 s.

Formulas and full tables: the 2026-11-16 edition.

Well established

  • Theory: JRSS-B 2012 (Thas et al.; 163 citations) with asymptotics, sandwich variance and simulations.
  • Generalises classic rank tests (Wilcoxon–Mann–Whitney, Kruskal–Wallis, Friedman). Our check: PI 0.749275, identical to WMW.
  • Small-sample bias is known (Amorim et al. 2018) and has published fixes.
  • Package: pim 2.0.4 on CRAN, maintained by the Ghent group.
  • Known failure modes here: 18–35 firms per year, ties (many zero buybacks), collinear covariates.

A streaming within-block PIM fitter

A Rust core that batches permutation and bootstrap refits without rebuilding pair matrices; scales to thousands of Q2 programs.

Test. β and SE parity ≤ 1e-8 with pim on all 29 models; 10,000 permutations on 360 firm-years in < 2 s.

2 · Math · Effort M
Firm-clustered inference for PIMs

Firms repeat across years, but pim's sandwich treats firm-years as independent. Derive a two-way U-statistic variance.

Test. Simulation at our design: 95% coverage within ±1.5 points.
