Finding 1

The book is no steadier than one program of the same size.

Usual measures say about 3× steadier. Allowing for how volatility grows with size gives 0.99 (Corning mock) and 1.15 (MS mock).

Original chart from the edition (tap)›
Finding 2

Part of the apparent diversification is just measurement noise.

On the mocks' noise-free true levels, the average-CV effect falls from 3.36 to 1.54 (Corning mock).

Finding 3

Honest miss: a plain correlation check finds hidden groups better.

Correlation puts all 9 planted groups at the 100th percentile. The portfolio effect scatters them from the 0th to the 81st.

Original chart from the edition (tap)›
  1. Fit one line across programs: log variance against log mean spend (Taylor's power law). Its slope is z.
  2. Extrapolate the line to the size of the whole book: that is the volatility one program that big would have.
  3. Compare with the book's real volatility. Above 1 means real diversification.
  4. If z = 2 this equals the usual average-CV measure. Here z is 1.67 (Corning mock) and 1.77 (MS mock), so big programs are calmer and the usual measure overstates.
  5. Fast: one log-log regression, 0.1 ms per portfolio.

Formulas and full tables: the 2026-11-16 edition.

Promising but thin

  • Taylor's law is solid. Reported since 1961 across insects, fish and plants; Cohen & Xu (PNAS 2015) show why it appears.
  • The portfolio metric is thin. One methods paper (Anderson, Cooper & Dulvy 2013; 88 citations). Across 51 metapopulations the usual measure was stronger in 84%, by up to 7×.
  • Package: v0.1.0, single author, no tests. Our reproduction of its vignette matched exactly (1.412).
  • Known failure: it extrapolates about two orders of magnitude; the quadratic variant blew up here (34.7).
1 · Engineering · Effort S–M
Ragged-panel port, R as oracle

ecofolio needs every program in every year, so 24–28% of programs were dropped. A NumPy/Rust port could keep entrants and exiters.

Test. Parity ≤ 1e-10 with ecofolio on 1,000 random subsets; 999-shuffle null on 1,404 programs in < 1 s.

2 · Math · Effort M · ★ Top pick
A noise-corrected Taylor's law and portfolio effect

Budget and actual are two noisy readings of each program. Their disagreement estimates the noise, which can be removed before fitting z.

Test. On both mocks plus 20 new-seed mocks, the corrected effect is closer to the true-level effect in ≥ 80% of cells. Pre-registered.

3 · New use · Effort S
Stability cost of FY2026 decision packages

Recompute z and the effect under the minimum, current and enhanced package scenarios.

Test. Change per scenario against 999 random same-cost package swaps, stated in advance.
