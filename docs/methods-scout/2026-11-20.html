Methods scout

Two borrowed tools, each with a full research card. From climate dynamics: information flow, which measures how much one series changes the uncertainty of another, with a sign. From organization science and agronomy: necessary-condition ceilings, which ask what a stress result rules out rather than what it predicts on average.

● Q1: spread moves raise fails' uncertainty (τ +1.7%, p = 0.033) and damp DVP volume's (τ −3.6%, p = 0.013); none survives Holm, and the usual tests overstate weekly links (14–67% false alarms at 5%). ● Q3: the stressed CET1 trough caps payouts beyond what correlation explains (d 0.25 vs 0.21, p = 0.012); the drawdown's "necessity" is correlation-made; the SCB binds for 1.5% of firm-years.

The short version

  • Liang–Kleeman information flow (LK_Info_flow) · climate dynamics · Q1 · interesting · home field: contested (Liang 2021 network reproduced within 0.4 pp; supported for small linear systems, disputed for nonlinear). Adds size and sign to Granger links; its shipped test is the Granger t-test and is oversized on our data. Miss: no forecasting gain.
  • Necessary Condition Analysis (R NCA) · organization science / agronomy · Q3 · interesting · home field: contested (example reproduced exactly; our size check confirms the critique of its default test). With a correlation-matched null, separates a real gate (CET1 trough) from a correlation-made one (drawdown). Misses: fragile to the best-capitalized 2% for total payout; loose as an envelope.
