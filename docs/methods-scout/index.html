Ad hoc exotic run: codyn rank clocks (ecology) and Lovell proportionality (compositional genomics), aimed at Q1

PROXY Y-9C, same panel as 11-11. US dealer volumes co-move (Schluter VR = 4.45 vs a cyclic-shift null of 0.44–2.00); foreign IHC volumes do not (VR = 0.73, inside the null). Rank clocks make the GS→JPM equity-revenue swap visible. JPM–GS proportionality is −0.698 and gets stronger after the market is removed (−0.882, BH q = 0.036); JPM–MS does not. MaxLIPO is on the watchlist as the MD’s example, not a pick.

MARSS state-space share decomposition and InspectChangepoint sparse change points, aimed at Q1 (dealer share: market vs sticky)

PROXY data (Y-9C), because top-25 prime-broker data isn't in Drive. Foreign-bank IHCs' share of 14-dealer trading assets fell from 13.8% to 5.9% since 2016, almost all of it sticky. In big-4 equity trading revenue, market factors drive most quarterly swings, except a sticky GS→JPM shift (corr −0.79). No share "break" survives a persistence-matched null.

partykit trees (ctree + MOB) and changeforest change points

The VIX→credit-spread link roughly doubles when NFCI > 0.06, and calendar time stops mattering. Regime trees did not forecast 6-month spread changes out of sample. changeforest finds volatility and correlation regimes that mean-change methods miss, including the 2022 stock–rate correlation flip, but its break count is seed-sensitive.
