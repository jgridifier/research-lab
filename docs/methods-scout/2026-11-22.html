Methods scout

Two borrowed tools, each with a full research card. From astrostatistics: interpolated cross-correlation, the method that times a quasar's light echo from two telescopes' uneven records, here timing Form PF borrowing against weekly repo. From hydrology: L-moment regional frequency analysis, the way hydrologists estimate a rare flood at a river with ten years of data by pooling similar rivers, here pooling short program budgets.

● Q1: OFR DVP repo volume follows Form PF reverse-repo borrowing by about six weeks (centroid +39 d, r 0.92, p = 0.001 under two nulls); the home-field rule and monthly Bartlett bands find "lags" in independent series 54–99% of the time. ● Q2 (SYNTHETIC): the heterogeneity test picks stated function as the valid pooling unit (median H1 ≈ 0 vs 2.3–8.3 for other groupings); pooling cuts the q90 error by a quarter, no more than category pooling.

The short version

  • Interpolated cross-correlation with RSS lag distributions (PyCCF) · astrostatistics · Q1 · interesting · home field: established with caveats (port matches PyCCF to 2×10⁻¹³ and runs 10× faster; error bars conservative at home, slightly tight on our cadence). Lags in days without regridding; with a persistence-matched null, one of five pairs survives. Miss: no forecasting gain over ARDL or SARIMAX.
  • L-moment regional frequency analysis (lmomRFA) · hydrology · Q2 · interesting · home field: established with known limits (Hosking's Cascades example reproduced exactly; low power on short, correlated records). H1 identifies the valid pooling unit and ranks mixed regions better than Brown–Forsythe. Misses: no power edge at matched size; ties with category pooling; weak mislabel detection.
