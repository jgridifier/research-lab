Ad-hoc Quant & Bank-Fundamentals Research

A personal research notebook exploring non-embedding financial-statement forecasting, FR Y-9C bank holding company panels, and DFM–Sinkhorn nowcasting methods. Separate from any Portfolios or production pipeline work.

What this lab is. Ad-hoc quant research — lit synthesis, teaching notes, and method sketches — on bank fundamentals forecasting and adjacent quantitative problems. All work is independently motivated; no content here constitutes financial advice or employer output.

Research Tracks

Track 1
Financial Statement Forecasting

Non-embedding methods for joint multi-item forecasting of bank holding company income statements and balance sheets — FR Y-9C panel with Yahoo smoke. Covers empirical-Bayes panels, chained GBM, MIDAS, MinT reconciliation.

Track 2
Y-9C / Yahoo Bank Panel

Research data path for bank statement forecasting: FFIEC NIC FR Y-9C bulk downloads and Yahoo Finance quarterly smoke. Panel assembly scripts, alignment notes, and known quirks.

Track 3
DFM × Sinkhorn Nowcasting

Literature exploration at the intersection of dynamic factor models (ragged-edge nowcasting) and Sinkhorn / entropic optimal transport as an observation or imputation loss. Thin but real intersection; anchored by Spelta et al. (JRSS-A 2025).

About this Lab

Scope

This lab collects independent research on quantitative methods for bank fundamentals and macroeconomic nowcasting. The primary application is the FR Y-9C BHC consolidated reporting panel (FFIEC NIC bulk downloads), with Yahoo Finance quarterly data as a cheap smoke-test path.

What is not here

  • Production Portfolios or ETF pipeline work (see usa-etf-feature-pipeline).
  • Raw panel CSVs, HF checkpoints, or secrets.
  • Gated data or paywalled assets.
  • Forma-release or proforma-20q production outputs.

How to navigate

Each track has an overview page, one or more teaching pages (method intuition, worked sketches), and lit memos (structured literature synthesis). Use the top nav to jump between tracks.
