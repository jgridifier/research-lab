Financial Statement Forecasting

Non-embedding methods for joint multi-item forecasting of bank holding company statements on the FR Y-9C panel. Empirical-Bayes dynamic panels, chained GBM, MIDAS mixed-frequency, and MinT reconciliation.

Overview

Valuation, credit, and stress testing need joint paths for many statement lines over multi-quarter horizons — not only next-quarter EPS. This track covers transparent, testable, non-embedding methods that work on the FR Y-9C panel without gated fine-tuned transformer weights.

Core method families

  • Empirical-Bayes dynamic panels — Liu–Moon–Schorfheide Tweedie posterior means for heterogeneous BHC coefficients; probabilistic PPNR forecasts.
  • Chained multi-output GBM — Geertsema–Lu–Ma ordering of statement lines so upstream predictions become downstream features; soft identity approximation.
  • MIDAS / sg-LASSO MIDAS — Ball–Ghysels and Babii et al. for connecting Yahoo daily smoke to quarterly Y-9C targets.
  • Forecast reconciliation (MinT) — Wickramasuriya et al. / Girolimetto–Di Fonzo projection onto the accounting-identity constraint set.
  • Multitask FFNN — Alberg–Lipton small networks sharing strength across MDRMs.
