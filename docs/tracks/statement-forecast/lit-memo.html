Non-embedding Financial-Statement Forecasting: Literature Map

Application panel: FR Y-9C (BHC consolidated) + Yahoo smoke. Date: 2026-09-23. Research memo only — not investment advice. Separate from Portfolios.

Do not claim: Forma OOS numbers transfer to Y-9C. All rankings and "what we'd try" synthesized via NVIDIA NIM glm-5.3-flash; paper anchors from manual WebSearch/WebFetch/arXiv HTML.

1. Scope

In scope: Joint (or multi-item) forecasting of complete-ish statements — income, balance sheet, CF-like flows — at horizons ~1–20 quarters, on a panel of many firms/BHCs, with preference for methods that can respect accounting identities / coherence.

Primary application panel: FR Y-9C bank holding company consolidated reports via FFIEC NIC BHCF bulk zips (preferred data path).
Smoke path: Yahoo Finance quarterly revenue/expense for BHC tickers in a reporting hierarchy.

Method families included:

  1. Classical / statistical: VAR/VECM, DFM/factor, state-space/Kalman, Bayesian hierarchical / empirical-Bayes dynamic panels, MIDAS / mixed-frequency, nowcasting panels.
  2. Structured ML (non-LLM backbone): GBM/RF, elastic-net / sg-LASSO, GPs, quantile / distributional regression, multi-task / multi-output, small multitask nets (not foundation FT).
  3. Probabilistic / coherent: multivariate densities, quantile stacks, forecast reconciliation (MinT / general linear constraints).
  4. LLM-as-feature-guide only (prompted sentiment as exogenous) — not FT embeddings.

Excluded: Large embedding / transformer foundation models trained or heavily fine-tuned on statements (Forma-class) — contrast only. Pure price/return alpha ML.

2. Cluster map

Cluster A — Y-9C / BHC Panel Anchors

PaperClaimFit
Liu, Moon, Schorfheide — Forecasting with Dynamic Panel Data Models, NBER w25102 (2018) Empirical-Bayes / Tweedie posterior means for heterogeneous dynamic-panel coefficients; forecasts BHC PPNR from Y-9C under actual vs severely adverse macros. #1 NIM pick. Direct Y-9C PPNR application; hierarchical shrinkage for unbalanced BHC panel; posterior predictive = probabilistic.
Mitra et al. — Interest rate uncertainty and the predictability of bank revenues, Journal of Forecasting Extends Liu-style Bayes panel PPNR with rate-uncertainty; NII vs noninterest split. Stress-test relevant exogenous for Y-9C revenue blocks.
Medeiros-style firm VAR/VECM statement systems Cointegrated statement systems; impulse responses. Single-firm / small-N intuition for identities; not primary for large BHC panel.

Cluster B — Full / Multi-item Statement ML

PaperClaimFit
Geertsema, Lu, Ma — Projecting Financial Statements with AI (SSRN 5039433) Multi-target chained GBM for interdependent IS+BS items; articulation via chain order. #2 NIM pick. Closest non-transformer full-statement baseline.
Alberg & Lipton (2017/18); Chauhan, Alberg, Lipton (ICML 2020) Joint multi-fundamental forecasts with uncertainty via multitask FFNN/LSTM. #6 NIM pick. Classical multitask net benchmark (not foundation FT).
Divo et al. — Forecasting Company Fundamentals, TMLR 2025 Benchmark of ~24 models on selected fundamentals; open code (GitHub). Protocol / baseline zoo for Yahoo smoke before Y-9C scale.
Chen, Cho, Dou, Lev (JAR 2022) — RF/GBM on detailed financials Earnings-change direction from rich XBRL/financial features. Item-level tree baselines on Y-9C MDRMs.

Cluster C — Cross-section Earnings Classics

PaperClaimFit
Hou, van Dijk, Zhang (JAE 2012) Cross-sectional earnings model for ICC; coverage vs analysts. Foundational earnings forecast anchor; map to BHCK income lines.
Li & Mohanram (RAST 2014) EP/RI cross-section vs HVZ for ICC. Parsimonious cross-section competitors.
Fairfield, Sweeney, Yohn / Nissim–Penman Disaggregation & ratio mean-reversion. Feature design for Y-9C ratios.
Fama–French profitability forecasting (JB 2000; JF 2006) Cross-section profitability predictability. Mean-reversion baselines.

Cluster D — Mixed-frequency / Nowcasting

PaperClaimFit
Ball & Ghysels (Management Science 2018) — MIDAS earnings MIDAS earnings; combine with analysts; daily→quarterly. #5 NIM pick. Yahoo daily → quarterly Y-9C smoke.
Babii, Ball, Ghysels, Striaukas — Panel MIDAS sg-LASSO (arXiv 2307.02673) Sparse-group LASSO MIDAS panel nowcasts P/E & earnings with news+macro. #4 NIM pick. Structured high-dim mixed-freq for many BHCs.

Cluster E — Coherence / Reconciliation

PaperClaimFit
Wickramasuriya, Athanasopoulos, Hyndman — MinT (JASA 2019) Trace-minimization reconciliation for hierarchical/grouped series. Post-process any base Y-9C forecasts to satisfy additivity. FoReco / hts R packages.
Girolimetto & Di Fonzo (Stat Methods Appl 2024; arXiv 2305.05330) Point & probabilistic reconciliation for general linear constraints. Accounting identities as signed linear constraints — native for Y-9C hierarchies.
Forma (Johnson et al., arXiv 2608.11327) — variance-weighted identity projection Reconciliation cost on statement forecasts (contrast). Proof that variance-weighted projection matters; do not adopt Forma FT path.

Cluster F — Distributional / Quantile

PaperClaimFit
Konstantinidi & Pope (CAR 2016) Quantile regressions → earnings distribution / risk metrics. Tail risk on Y-9C PPNR / NI; feed CRPS evaluation.

Cluster H — Parked / Deprioritized

ItemWhy parked
Forma tuple-set transformer + ProForma-20Q Specialist FT on Compustat statements; gated weights; not Y-9C MDRM schema; non-embedding path requested first.
Chronos / TimesFM / frontier LLM zero-shot statement prompts Weak change-space R² on Forma's own table; expensive; not identity-native.
Large FT embedding models without call-report FT track record Explicitly out of scope until FT workflow proven.

3. Recommended stack (Yahoo smoke → Y-9C bulk)

  1. Liu–Moon–Schorfheide EB dynamic panel (PPNR) — Hierarchical empirical-Bayes shrinkage across BHCs; needs BHCF quarterly PPNR + macros.
  2. Geertsema–Lu–Ma chained GBM — Multi-target chained boosting across IS+BS; needs full BHCF item set per BHC-quarter.
  3. MinT + Girolimetto–Di Fonzo reconciliation — Probabilistic post-layer enforcing Y-9C additivity; needs hierarchy metadata from BHCF.
  4. Babii et al. sg-LASSO MIDAS — Sparse mixed-frequency map from Yahoo daily → quarterly Y-9C; smoke + bulk dual use.
  5. Ball–Ghysels MIDAS — Classic daily→quarterly baseline for Yahoo smoke before scaling.
  6. Alberg–Lipton multitask FFNN — Joint multi-line neural benchmark (classical multitask, not foundation FT).
  7. (Honorable) Hou–van Dijk–Zhang / Li–Mohanram cross-section earnings — fast earnings-only baselines on BHCK income.
  8. (Honorable) Konstantinidi–Pope quantiles — distributional layer / CRPS targets on PPNR & NI.

Preferred data path: FFIEC NIC ReturnBHCFZipFiles?zipfilename=BHCF{YYYYMMDD}.zip → y9c/ scripts. Yahoo under yahoo_bank_quarterly/ is smoke only.

4. Not recommended now

  • Forma-class / large statement transformers and embedding FT without a validated call-report FT track record.
  • Zero-shot frontier LLMs as primary statement engines (cost, non-determinism, weak change-space skill).
  • Equal-weighted identity projection without predictive variances (Forma Table 3 warning).
  • Claiming Compustat/Forma OOS metrics transfer to Y-9C.

5. Open questions / next experiments

E1 — Yahoo smoke (Ball–Ghysels + sg-LASSO MIDAS). Forecast quarterly PPNR for ~20 large BHCs from Yahoo daily (yields, VIX, spreads); score vs BHCF actuals with rolling-origin RMSE/CRPS.

E2 — Bulk EB panel (Liu–Moon–Schorfheide). EB dynamic panel on all BHCF filers' PPNR; coverage & small-BHC shrinkage vs pooled OLS.

E3 — Articulation + reconciliation (chained GBM → MinT). Fit chained GBM on IS+BS MDRMs; apply MinT/Girolimetto–Di Fonzo; measure identity-violation reduction pre/post.

Additional: Map ProForma-style identity list → Y-9C MDRM linear constraints. Point-in-time / revision awareness for Y-9C restatements. Scenario conditioning (unemployment, FF rate, spreads) à la Liu stress path — without Forma pinned-future FT.
