Commercial paper maturity mix: burn-in gate

Each week, companies issue commercial paper with maturities from 1 day to 270 days. For four issuer types we track the mix, meaning the share of dollars in each of six maturity buckets. The question is whether next week's mix can be forecast, and whether optimal transport (OT) methods add anything.

The distance

We place the buckets on a log-days axis and compare two mixes by the squared 2-Wasserstein distance. That distance splits exactly into location, scale and shape parts:

W₂² = (m₁−m₂)² + (s₁−s₂)² + 2·s₁·s₂·(1−ρ)
