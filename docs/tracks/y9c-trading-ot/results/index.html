Design

  • Target. Quarterly BHCKA220, de-cumulated from year-to-date (Q1 as reported, no bridging over gaps), thousands of USD. Negative values kept; no logs.
  • Views. T1 industry total; T2 19 banks with |quarterly A220| ≥ $10m in all 16 burn-in quarters (2018Q1–2021Q4), chosen from burn-in data only; T3 banks with lagged trading assets ≥ $100m.
  • Timing. Burn-in 2018Q1–2021Q4; 18 one-step origins 2021Q4–2026Q1. A quarter is usable 7 days after its Y-9C due date.
  • Baselines. SAA-8, seasonal naive, ratio random walk, SES (α = 0.3), AR(1)+Q1, pooled quantile regression with FRED covariates, empirical-Bayes dynamic panel.
  • H1. Equal-weight quantile average (the 1-D W₂ barycenter) of the baselines, with a trim rule and one widening factor frozen at 2021Q4. No cross-validation.
  • H2. The forecast cross-section is a point on the W₂ geodesic from the Fréchet mean to the latest cross-section (scalar β). It maps to banks through a Gaussian-copula AR on probit ranks (scalar ρ).

Open design questions (must be resolved before scoring)

  • Q1-B6-burnin: B6 (eb_forecast_origin, unchanged default T=12) needs a complete 13-quarter window of the ratio, which begins 2018Q2 (first lagged denominator). It is therefore undefined at pseudo-origins 2020Q1-2021Q1: on the burn-in pseudo-OOS window (targets 2020Q2-2021Q4) B6 exists only for targets 2021Q3-2021Q4 (38 of 133 bank cases; same pattern on the cross-section). The prereg's trim rule, s selection and B* selection assume 133 cases for every member. Options: (a) matched cases only (all members on 2021Q3-Q4, 38 cases); (b) each member on its own available cases; (c) exclude B6 from M0 and from B* eligibility; (d) other. Must be fixed before the freeze; v1.1 history extension to 2008 would remove the problem if the burn-in moves.
  • Q2-B6-denominator: Prereg says B6 forecast = Tweedie posterior-mean ratio x TA_t (TA = trading assets, BHCK3545), but asset_ratio uses BHCK2170 total assets. Proposed: pass BHCK3545 into eb's total_assets slot (eb.py code unchanged). Confirm or choose total assets.
  • Q3-B6-residuals: B6 'empirical residual quantiles': proposed = in-sample one-step residuals over the 12-quarter EB window, reconstructed from the returned fit (12 per bank, so own errors).
  • Q4-pseudo-pool-scale: Pooled scaled errors at pseudo-origins before 2021Q4 would use s_i (2018Q1-2021Q4 MAD), i.e. data after the pseudo-origin. Proposed pooling scale = 1.4826 MAD over 2018Q1..min(origin, 2021Q4); scoring still uses frozen s_i.
  • Q5-CS-baselines: B*_CS: baselines on the cross-section are run in ratio space (centres and errors in r bp, pooled errors scaled by s_i^r; B5 pooled over C_(s+h) members; B6 level forecast / TA_t).
  • Q6-T1-baselines: T1 (H1d, secondary) has one series: with < 8 own errors there is nothing to pool, and B4's fallback B2 does not exist on levels. Proposed: own errors whatever their count (>= 2); B4 fallback = level random walk.
  • Q7-minor: Minor conventions: SES initialised at the first observed r and held when r is missing; U19 -> U99 interpolation holds the 0.05/0.95 values flat in the tails; at h = 4 B5 drops r_(t+h-4) (identical to r_t); trailing-8 MAD uses the last 8 observed r values.

Data: FFIEC NIC FR Y-9C bulk files (34 quarters). Macro: FRED VIXCLS, DGS10, SP500. Report-quarter backtest on the latest vintage; filing revisions are not modelled.
