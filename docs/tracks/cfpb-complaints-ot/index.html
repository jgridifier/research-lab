CFPB complaints × OT: Killed at burn-in (negative result)

Status: killed at burn-in. No out-of-sample run. The burn-in median next-month W₂² was 0.0064, below the half-sample noise floor of 0.0269 (ratio 0.237). By the pre-registered rule the object is killed: from one month to the next, the shape of complaints across companies moved less than two random halves of the same month differ. A forecast of that month-to-month change could not be told apart from sampling noise, so nothing was scored and no replacement object is proposed.

What was going to be forecast

Each month, the spread of complaint counts across companies in the CFPB consumer-complaint file: the equal-weighted distribution of log(1 + complaints) among companies with at least one complaint that month, with three pinned credit-bureau names left out. Two optimal-transport methods were pre-registered against simple baselines: an equal-weight W₂ barycenter of the baselines (the average of their quantile functions) and a Wasserstein autoregression of the monthly quantile functions. The plain-language explanation is on the learning page.

The half-sample distance is the noise floor: split each month's companies at random into two halves and measure how far apart their quantile functions are. If consecutive months are closer than that, the month-to-month signal is below the noise and there is nothing for a forecast to beat.
